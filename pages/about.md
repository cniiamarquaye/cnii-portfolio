  I work at the intersection of geospatial intelligence and environmental systems, building analysis that does not stop at the output but follows through to the insight.
</p>
<p class="mt-4 text-sm text-gray-600 dark:text-slate-400 leading-relaxed">
  My focus is on land and water systems: how they change under human and natural pressures, and what those changes mean for planning, management and sustainability. I hold a PhD from the University of Yamanashi, and my background spans marine science, environmental consulting and international research collaboration.
</p>
<p class="mt-4 text-sm text-gray-600 dark:text-slate-400 leading-relaxed">
  I build end-to-end geospatial workflows with ArcGIS Pro, QGIS, Google Earth Engine, Python and R, integrating remote sensing (including SAR), spatial analysis and machine learning into reproducible, scalable systems. My current focus is environmental modelling at the climate–land–water interface.
</p>
